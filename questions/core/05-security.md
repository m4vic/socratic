# Core Security

1. What is the most sensitive asset and where are the trust boundaries?
2. Who may perform each action, and where is authorization enforced?
3. How are external inputs, URLs, files, queries, and rendered content constrained?
4. How are secrets sourced, scoped, rotated, and kept out of logs?
5. What is the blast radius of compromise or automated abuse?
6. How would operators detect, investigate, contain, and recover from an incident?

Verify denied access, malicious inputs, secret exposure, dependency risk, transport controls, and audit evidence.
