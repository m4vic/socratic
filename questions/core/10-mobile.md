# Core Mobile and Offline

1. Which platforms, OS versions, devices, and accessibility settings are supported?
2. What works offline or on a flaky connection, and how are conflicts resolved?
3. How are local secrets and schema migrations protected?
4. What happens when the app backgrounds or loses permission mid-operation?
5. What launch, responsiveness, battery, and network budgets matter?
6. How are incompatible clients and bad releases halted or rolled back?

Verify offline transitions, conflict handling, denied permissions, migration, accessibility, and staged rollout.
