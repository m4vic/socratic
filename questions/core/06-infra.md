# Core Infrastructure and DevOps

1. Where does it run, and how does a commit become a deployed artifact?
2. How close are development, staging, and production environments?
3. What health checks, rollout, rollback, and migration ordering are required?
4. What is publicly reachable, and how are secrets and configuration delivered?
5. What fails first at higher load, and what single points of failure remain?
6. Can the environment be rebuilt reproducibly and affordably?

Verify deployment, rollback, failed health checks, network exposure, and reproducible environment creation.
