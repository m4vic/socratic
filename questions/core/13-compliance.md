# Core Compliance, Legal, and Ethics

1. What personal or regulated data is processed, where, and why is it necessary?
2. What consent, residency, retention, export, and deletion obligations apply?
3. Which vendors or model providers receive the data, under what agreement?
4. What breach, audit, access-review, and record-keeping duties apply?
5. Are dependency, dataset, and generated-output rights compatible with distribution?
6. Could the system materially harm or discriminate, and where is human recourse required?

Escalate legal judgments to counsel. Verify the actual data flow, deletion path, vendor list, licenses, and policy accuracy.
