# Product discovery: sources

**Primary sources:**

- Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test* — how to ask about a problem without
  biasing the answer: talk about their life rather than your idea, ask about
  past behavior rather than future intent, and treat compliments as noise.
- Marty Cagan, *Inspired* — product discovery as risk reduction across value,
  usability, feasibility, and business viability, and why validation belongs
  before delivery rather than after.

**Supporting material:**

- Teresa Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits* — sustaining customer contact
  as a habit rather than a phase, and mapping opportunities to outcomes.
- Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup* — the smallest artifact that produces a real
  signal, and explicit criteria for persevering or stopping.

This pack answers a question the engineering packs assume is already settled:
whether the thing should exist. It pairs naturally with the Requirements and
Product/UX domains and is usually loaded before, not alongside, a design pack.

A future `full.md` could add decision clusters for interview scripting and
note-taking, pricing signal, opportunity solution trees, concierge and
Wizard-of-Oz test design, and distinguishing retention from novelty in early
usage data. Add a card only when it changes a real decision or verification
step.
